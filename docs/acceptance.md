## What acceptance here does not mean

- `schema-invalid` counts cells that do not carry the fields `spec/schema.json` requires. It is computed by `ci/validate_cells.py`, the same ruler `docs/cell-validation.md` is written from, and it is printed beside each port's own self-reported count because a window grading its own homework is the failure this leg exists to catch.

A row is 'OK' when the required cells exist on the canonical face, not when the port's answers are correct - correctness against the spec is the sealed expectation table's job (`docs/expected-summary.md`), and agreement between ports is the gate's job (`docs/STATUS.md`). A port that ran one entry out of its pinned list shows ENTRY-GAP even with a full cell count, because an unexercised entry is a claim about the harness that the harness did not test.

- `schema-invalid` says nothing about coverage. Nine of the ten fields the gate scores as a verdict are not in `spec/schema.json`'s required list, so a port can be conforming and silent in the same minute. The blanks are `docs/coverage.md`'s subject, and both numbers belong beside any quotation of either.
