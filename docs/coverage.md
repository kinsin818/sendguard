## What a blank is, and what it is not

- A blank is **not** an agreement: the gate compares only the ports that recorded a field, and a cell where
  five of six recorded still counts as `VERDICT-NOT-RECORDED` for the sixth (121 such field-cells in this run).
- A blank is **not yet** distinguishable from 'this field does not apply to this case group'. The frozen contract
  lists verdict fields per checker answer but never says which of them apply to which case group, so this
  report publishes every blank and judges none of them. Deciding which blanks are legal is a contract
  change (the current sealed round is the eighth); filling a blank is the port's own work.
